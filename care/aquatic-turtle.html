Red-eared sliders and other water turtles are active and long-lived, often 20-40 years. They grow larger than most people expect and need lots of water.

Quick facts

  • Adult size 8-12 inches; lifespan 20-40+ years.
  • Never release a turtle outdoors. It harms native wildlife and is illegal in California.

Habitat

  • Allow about 10 gallons of water per inch of shell; adults need 75-120+ gallons.
  • Water depth at least 1.5-2 times shell length.
  • A dry basking dock the turtle can climb fully out onto.
  • Strong filter rated for 2-3 times the tank size; bare bottom or large smooth rocks.

Heat & light

  • Water 75-80°F with a guarded heater; basking spot 90-95°F.
  • T5 HO UVB over the basking dock, 12-18 inches away; replace every 6-12 months.
  • No glass or plastic between the UVB and the turtle. 12-14 hours of light a day.

Diet & feeding

  • Juveniles: feed daily. Adults: every 2-3 days.
  • Turtle pellets plus leafy greens and water plants; adults eat more greens.
  • Occasional treats: earthworms, shrimp, feeder insects.
  • Feed in a separate tub to keep the main tank cleaner.

Cleaning & health

  • Change 25-50% of the water weekly; use dechlorinator and match temperature.
  • Turtles can carry Salmonella: wash hands after any contact; keep away from kitchen sinks.
  • See a reptile vet for: puffy or closed eyes, floating crooked, soft shell, shell pits or white patches.

Starter checklist

  • Large tank or tub, canister or strong filter, submersible heater, basking dock.
  • Basking bulb, T5 HO UVB, thermometers, dechlorinator, pellets, test kit.

Sources: VCA Animal Hospitals - Housing Aquatic Turtles, ReptiFiles - Red Eared Slider Care Sheet (PDF)

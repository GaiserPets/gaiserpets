African Dwarf Frog Care Sheet

African dwarf frogs are small, peaceful frogs that live fully underwater. They are social, slow eaters and do best in small groups.

Quick facts

  • Adult size 1.5-2.5 inches; lifespan about 5 years or more.
  • Fully aquatic; they breathe air at the surface.
  • Front feet are webbed. Clawed frogs look similar but are a different, much larger species.

Habitat

  • At least 10 gallons for 2-4 frogs; keep in groups of 2 or more.
  • Water about 12 inches deep so they can swim yet reach the surface easily.
  • Gentle filter, smooth gravel or sand, plants and caves to hide in.
  • Tight lid; frogs can climb out and dry out within minutes.

Water

  • Heater set to 75-80°F (never above 82°F); check with a thermometer.
  • Cycle the tank first: ammonia and nitrite 0, nitrate under 20 ppm.
  • Change 20-25% weekly with dechlorinated, temperature-matched water.

Diet & feeding

  • Feed 2-3 times a week: sinking frog pellets, frozen bloodworms or brine shrimp.
  • They are slow, nearly blind eaters; use a feeding dish or turkey baster to target-feed.
  • Fast tankmates may steal food; watch that each frog eats.

Tankmates & handling

  • Only small, peaceful fish; no bettas or fish big enough to eat a frog.
  • Avoid handling; use a net only when moving them.

Health warning signs

  • See an exotic vet for: bloating, cloudy or peeling skin, red spots, weight loss, floating constantly.
  • Never use copper-based medications in a frog tank.

Starter checklist

  • 10+ gallon tank with lid, gentle filter, heater, thermometer, dechlorinator, test kit.
  • Smooth gravel or sand, plants, caves, frog pellets, frozen foods, feeding dish.

Sources: Colorado Exotic Animal Hospital - Basic Care: African Dwarf Frog, Vet Verified - A Comprehensive Guide to African Dwarf Frogs, California Code of Regulations Title 14 §671 (Xenopus restricted)

Goldfish are hardy, friendly fish that can live 10-20 years. They grow big and make a lot of waste, so they need far more room than a bowl.

Quick facts

  • Fancy goldfish reach 6-8 inches; comets and commons 6-10+ inches.
  • Lifespan 10-20 years with good care.
  • Never release goldfish into creeks, ditches or lakes. It is harmful and illegal.

Aquarium setup

  • No bowls or tanks under 10 gallons. Adults need 30+ gallons, plus about 10 more for each extra fish.
  • Comets and commons outgrow most tanks; a pond is best for them as adults.
  • Strong filter and an air stone; goldfish need lots of oxygen.
  • Smooth gravel or sand and sturdy plants; skip sharp decor that tears fins.

Water

  • Usually no heater: 65-75°F is ideal. Keep the tank out of sunny windows.
  • Cycle the tank first: ammonia 0, nitrite 0, nitrate under 20-40 ppm.
  • Change 25-30% weekly with dechlorinated, temperature-matched water.

Diet & feeding

  • Sinking goldfish pellets or flakes once or twice a day; what they eat in 1-2 minutes.
  • Add peas (skins off), blanched greens or frozen brine shrimp a few times a week.
  • Don't mix with tropical fish; they need different temperatures.

Pond goldfish

  • Ponds need good filtration, shade, and an air pump in summer.
  • Stop feeding when the water drops below about 50°F. See our Koi & Pond Fish sheet.

Health & checklist

  • Ask us or a fish vet about: floating upside down, clamped fins, white spots, red streaks.
  • Buy: large tank, filter, air pump and stone, dechlorinator, test kit, gravel vacuum, food.

Sources: Chewy Education - What Size Goldfish Tank Do I Need?, Merck Veterinary Manual - Disorders and Diseases of Fish (owner version), VIN - Water Quality in Koi Practice

Cockatiels are affectionate, whistling members of the cockatoo family. They bond closely with people and can live 15-20+ years.

Quick facts

  • About 12-13 inches long; lifespan 15-20+ years.
  • Gentle and social; they need daily time with you or another cockatiel.

Cage

  • Minimum 24" x 24" x 30" tall; bigger is better. Bar spacing 1/2 to 5/8 inch.
  • Natural wood perches of different widths; toys to chew, shred and forage.
  • Daily supervised time out of the cage to stretch and fly.

Diet

  • Pellets about 60-80% of the diet; veggies such as greens, broccoli, carrot and sweet potato.
  • Seed, millet and fruit are treats or training rewards.
  • Fresh water daily. See our Bird Safe & Toxic Foods handout.

Home safety

  • Nonstick (PTFE) cookware and self-cleaning ovens can release fumes that kill birds.
  • No smoke, aerosols or plug-in fresheners. Watch for open doors, ceiling fans and hot stoves.
  • Keep out of hot windows; cockatiels struggle in summer heat without AC.

Sleep & behavior

  • 10-12 hours of sleep. A small night light helps prevent 'night frights' (thrashing in the dark).
  • Powder dust is normal for cockatiels; regular baths or misting help.

Health warning signs

  • Birds hide illness. See an avian vet for: fluffed and sleepy, tail bobbing, sitting on the floor.
  • Also: changes in droppings, discharge from eyes or nose, not eating, feather plucking.

Starter checklist

  • Cage, perches, dishes, toys, night light, cage cover, bird bath or mister.
  • Pellets, small amount of seed, cuttlebone, bird-safe cleaner.

Sources: Royal Veterinary College - Cockatiel Care (2023), VCA Animal Hospitals - Teflon (PTFE) Poisoning in Birds

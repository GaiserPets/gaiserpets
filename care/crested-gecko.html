Crested geckos are easygoing tree-dwelling geckos that eat a ready-made fruit diet. Their biggest danger in Yuba City is summer heat.

Quick facts

  • Adult size 7-9 inches; lifespan 15-20 years.
  • Gentle but jumpy; most active at night.
  • A dropped tail never grows back, so handle gently.

Habitat

  • Adult minimum 18" x 18" x 24" tall; taller and bigger is better.
  • Moisture-holding substrate such as coconut fiber or a soil mix.
  • Lots of branches, vines and leaves (live or artificial) to climb and hide in.

Temperature & light

  • Keep at 72-80°F by day and 65-75°F at night.
  • Over 85°F can be deadly. In summer keep the room air-conditioned; never put the tank in a window.
  • Low-strength UVB (T5 5-6%) on a 12-hour timer is helpful; replace every 6-12 months.

Humidity & water

  • Mist in the evening to about 70-80%; let it dry to about 50-60% by day.
  • Geckos drink droplets from leaves; also offer a small water dish.
  • Check humidity with a digital hygrometer.

Diet & feeding

  • Main diet: complete crested gecko powder diet mixed with water, per the label.
  • Juveniles: fresh diet daily. Adults: 3 times a week.
  • Remove old diet within 24-48 hours.
  • Optional: dusted small insects once a week.

Handling & health

  • Let it walk hand to hand; never grab the tail.
  • See a reptile vet for: weight loss, wavy or bent jaw or spine, stuck shed, refusing food over 2 weeks.

Starter checklist

  • 18x18x24 or larger enclosure, substrate, branches and plants, mister or spray bottle.
  • Digital thermometer/hygrometer, low UVB fixture, complete diet powder, feeding ledge or cups.

Sources: ReptiFiles - Crested Gecko Care Sheet (PDF), Royal Veterinary College - Crested Gecko Care (2024)

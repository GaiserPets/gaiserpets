Russian tortoises are small, tough, busy diggers that can live 40-50+ years. Plan for a big enclosure and outdoor time in good weather.

Quick facts

  • Adult size 5-10 inches; lifespan 40-50+ years.
  • Active, curious and strong diggers; best kept alone or in roomy groups.

Habitat

  • Indoor adult minimum 7' x 3.5' floor (about 24 sq ft); juveniles 4' x 2' to start.
  • Substrate: soil-sand mix 6+ inches deep (4+ for babies) so it can dig.
  • Hides, flat rocks, and a shallow water dish no deeper than its knees.
  • Outdoor pens: walls sunk 12+ inches deep, secure top, shade, and a burrow.

Heat & light

  • Basking spot 95°F; cool side 75-85°F; nights 70-75°F indoors.
  • T5 HO UVB (10-12%) over about 1/3 of the enclosure; replace every 6-12 months.
  • Outdoors in summer, give deep shade and damp burrows; bring indoors in extreme heat or frost.

Diet & water

  • Mostly weeds and leafy greens: dandelion, endive, escarole, clover, plantain, mulberry leaves.
  • Juveniles eat daily; adults a shell-sized portion daily. Fruit and protein are rare treats only.
  • Dust food with calcium; offer a cuttlebone. Soak babies 10-15 minutes twice a week.

Health & care

  • Spot-clean daily; replace indoor substrate every 3-6 months. Wash hands after handling.
  • Ask a reptile vet before letting your tortoise hibernate (brumate).
  • See a reptile vet for: swollen or closed eyes, runny nose, soft or lumpy shell, not eating.

Starter checklist

  • Large tortoise table or enclosure, T5 HO UVB, basking bulb, thermostat or dimmer, thermometers.
  • Diggable substrate, hides, shallow water dish, calcium powder, cuttlebone, fresh greens.

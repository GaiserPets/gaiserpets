Bearded dragons are calm, curious lizards that make great first reptiles. With good heat, strong UVB and a varied diet they can live 10-15 years.

Quick facts

  • Adult size 18-24 inches; lifespan 10-15 years, sometimes 20.
  • Usually gentle and easy to handle once settled in.
  • House one dragon per enclosure; adults fight or bully each other.

Habitat

  • Adult minimum 4' x 2' x 2' (120 gallons); 6' long is even better.
  • Babies and juveniles: paper towel, tile or shelf liner (no loose substrate).
  • Adults: tile, or a packed soil-sand mix 4+ inches deep. Avoid calcium sand and walnut shell.
  • Add a hide, a sturdy basking platform, and branches or rocks to climb.

Heat & light

  • Basking surface 105-110°F; cool side 75-85°F. Check with a digital probe or temp gun.
  • Lights off at night; nights down to 65°F are fine.
  • Linear T5 HO UVB (10-12%) over about half the length; replace every 6-12 months.
  • 12-14 hours of light a day on a timer; put heat lamps on a thermostat or dimmer.

Diet & supplements

  • Babies (0-6 months): insects twice daily plus fresh chopped greens daily.
  • Juveniles (6-12 months): insects once daily, greens daily.
  • Adults: insects 2-3 times a week; greens daily make up most of the diet.
  • Dust insects with calcium (no D3) most feedings and a multivitamin about once a week.
  • Shallow water dish daily; mist greens or lightly mist 1-2 times a week. Humidity 30-60%.

Cleaning & handling

  • Spot-clean poop and old food daily; deep-clean the enclosure monthly.
  • Scoop from underneath and support the whole body. Wash hands after (Salmonella).

Health warning signs

  • See a reptile vet for: not eating over 2 weeks (outside brumation), swollen jaw or legs, tremors, limping.
  • Also: runny poop over 2 days, sunken eyes, mouth gaping at rest, or stuck shed on toes.

Starter checklist

  • 120+ gallon enclosure, T5 HO UVB fixture and tube, basking bulb and fixture, thermostat or dimmer.
  • Digital thermometers or temp gun, timer, hides, basking platform, water dish.
  • Calcium and multivitamin powders, feeder insects, fresh greens.

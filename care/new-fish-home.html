The trip home is stressful for fish. These simple steps help your new fish settle in safely.

On the way home

  • Go straight home; bags can overheat or chill quickly in a car.
  • Keep the bag in a dark, insulated spot such as a box or cooler.
  • In summer never leave fish in a hot car, even for a few minutes.

Acclimating

  • Turn the tank lights off.
  • Float the sealed bag in the tank for 15-20 minutes to match temperature.
  • Open the bag and add about 1/2 cup of tank water every 5 minutes, 3-4 times.
  • Net the fish into the tank. Pour the bag water down the sink, not into your tank.

The first day

  • Leave the lights off for the rest of the day so the fish can settle.
  • Don't feed on the first day; start with a small meal the next day.
  • It's normal for new fish to hide or have pale color for a day or two.

The first weeks

  • Watch for white spots, clamped fins, gasping or not eating.
  • Test your water a few days after adding fish.
  • Add new fish slowly: a few at a time, 1-2 weeks apart.

Leopard geckos are gentle, nocturnal lizards from dry, rocky grasslands. They are hardy, and with good care can live 15-20 years.

Quick facts

  • Adult size 7-10 inches; lifespan 15-20+ years.
  • Calm, slow-moving and easy to handle once settled.
  • Keep males apart; they fight. Most geckos do best housed alone.

Habitat

  • Adult minimum 36" x 18" x 18" (about 40-gallon breeder); bigger is better.
  • Juveniles: paper towel or tile. Adults: tile, or a packed soil-sand mix.
  • Three hides: warm, cool, and a humid hide with damp moss.
  • Add rocks, branches and plants so it can climb and explore.

Heat & light

  • Warm basking surface 90-95°F; cool side 72-77°F.
  • Heat from above with a heat lamp on a thermostat; use a mat only with a thermostat.
  • Nights can drop to 65-70°F; turn the lights off.
  • Low-strength UVB (T5 5-7%) helps; replace every 6-12 months.

Humidity & water

  • Room humidity 30-40%; keep the humid hide moss damp at all times.
  • Shallow dish of fresh water, changed daily.

Diet & feeding

  • Juveniles: small insects daily. Adults: insects every 2-3 days.
  • Variety: crickets, dubia, mealworms; superworms and hornworms as treats.
  • Dust with calcium most feedings and a multivitamin about once a week.
  • Remove uneaten crickets so they don't nibble your gecko.

Handling & health

  • Never grab the tail; it can drop off and grows back looking different.
  • See a reptile vet for: weight loss or thin tail, stuck shed on toes, wobbly walking, runny poop.

Starter checklist

  • 40-gallon breeder or larger enclosure, heat lamp, thermostat, digital thermometers.
  • Three hides, moss, water dish, low-strength UVB, calcium and multivitamin powders, insects.

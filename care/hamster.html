Hamsters are busy, nighttime burrowers that need much more room than most pet-store cages. Give them deep bedding, a big wheel and a quiet spot.

Quick facts

  • Syrian hamsters: 6-7 inches. Dwarf hamsters: 2-4 inches. Lifespan 1.5-3 years.
  • Syrian hamsters must live alone. Dwarfs are usually happiest alone too.
  • Most active at dusk and night; don't wake them to play.

Habitat

  • Syrian minimum 900 sq in of unbroken floor (about 40" x 22"); dwarf minimum 700 sq in.
  • Solid floor, no wire floors or small multi-level cages.
  • Bedding 10+ inches deep for Syrians (6-8 for dwarfs): paper-based, never pine or cedar.
  • Several hides, chews, sand bath, and a solid wheel: 11-12"+ for Syrians, 8"+ for dwarfs.

Temperature

  • Keep at 65-75°F. Hamsters overheat fast above 80°F.
  • Keep the cage out of sun and away from windows; no heat in summer without AC.

Diet & water

  • Hamster pellets or lab blocks as the base, measured daily.
  • Small pieces of fresh veggies a few times a week; seeds and fruit as tiny treats.
  • Water bottle and a dish, changed daily.

Handling & cleaning

  • Let it sniff your hand, then scoop it up in cupped hands, low to the ground.
  • Spot-clean pee corners weekly; don't replace all the bedding at once.

Health warning signs

  • See an exotic vet for: wet, dirty tail or diarrhea (urgent), weight loss, overgrown teeth.
  • Also: lumps, hair loss, crusty eyes, wheezing, or limping.

Starter checklist

  • Large bin cage or tank with mesh lid, deep paper bedding, solid wheel, hides.
  • Water bottle, food dish, pellets, chews, sand bath.

Sources: PetMD - Hamster Cages, Royal Veterinary College - Syrian Hamster Care Advice, VCA Animal Hospitals - Owning a Pet Hamster
